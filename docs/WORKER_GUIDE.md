# Guide for workers

## Getting it on your phone

1. Open the address the office gave you in your phone's browser and add `/m` to the end, for example `https://portal.example.com.au/m`.
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. In the browser's menu choose **Add to Home Screen** (iPhone) or **Install app** (Android). It then opens like an app.

There is no app to download from a store.

## The tabs along the bottom

- **Home**: what you need to do today (accept a job, a timesheet day not sent, where you are signed on).
- **My work**: today's job. Accept it or decline it with a reason, then mark it done.
- **Forms**: forms the office has set up, such as a pre-start. You can save a draft and finish later.
- **Scan**: point the camera at a site sign or an asset label. If the camera does not work in the app, use your phone's own camera, or type the code printed under the QR code.
- **More**: everything else you are allowed to see, such as **My timesheet**, **My leave**, **My payslips** and **My profile**.

## Signing on to a site

Scan the QR sign at the gate, answer the questions, and tap **Sign on**. To leave, tap **Sign off** on Home.

## Timesheets

Open **More > My timesheet**. Tap **Fill in** on each day you worked, enter your start, finish and break, and save. When the week is done tap **Send to the office**. If the office sends a day back, it shows on Home with the reason.

## Leave

Open **More > My leave**, choose the dates and the type of leave, and send it. You will see when it is approved or declined.

## If you lose signal

If you save a timesheet day or submit a form and there is no signal, the phone keeps it and shows **waiting to send** at the top. It is sent by itself when the signal is back. You can see what is waiting by tapping that label.

Two things need signal: taking photos in a form, and opening a form or timesheet for the first time. Open what you need before you go out of range.

## If something looks wrong

A message in a red box tells you what happened and usually what to do. If it says you do not have permission, ask the office: they decide what each person can see.
